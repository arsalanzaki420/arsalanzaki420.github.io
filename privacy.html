Privacy policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026.

Homeserver Drive Access is a private application used only by its owner. It accesses only the owner's own Google Drive files, at the owner's request, to copy and sync them with the owner's personal home server.

  • Data accessed: the owner's Google Drive files and folders.
  • Where it goes: only to the owner's own server. Nothing is sent to any other party.
  • No data is sold, shared, used for advertising, or used to train any model.
  • Access can be withdrawn at any time in Google Account settings (Security, Third-party apps and services).
